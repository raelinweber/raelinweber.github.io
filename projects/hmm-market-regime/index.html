01 · Research overview

What we investigated

Financial markets move through changing conditions, including periods of differing volatility and momentum. This project examined whether a Gaussian Hidden Markov Model (GHMM) could infer these latent market regimes and use the estimated stress probability to filter two rule-based strategies.

The model was designed as a regime-identification and risk-management filter, not as a model for predicting exact future prices.

02 · Methodology

How the framework worked

  • Historical daily S&P 500 index data from 2000 to 2024.
  • Three model inputs: 20-day realised volatility, RSI (14) and Rate of Change (14).
  • A seven-state Gaussian HMM, with states ranked by their mean volatility.
  • The combined probability of stress states was used as a filter. At a threshold of 0.5, the strategy moved to cash for the next trading day.
  • Two strategies were tested: a 50/200-day SMA crossover and a 50-day breakout.
  • Expanding-window annual walk-forward evaluation from 2010 to 2024, with training-only scaling and fitting, forward-filtered probabilities, a one-day execution delay and 5 basis points per position change.
Chronological validation was used to avoid training on future observations. Results were evaluated out of sample rather than using a random train/test split.
03 · Results

Performance comparison

The HMM filter reduced maximum drawdown and improved the reported Sharpe ratio for both tested strategies. The largest improvement was observed for the SMA crossover.

Statistical checks

  • Buy-and-hold remained stronger, with a Sharpe ratio of 0.758 and annualised return of 12.3%.
  • The SMA HMM variant recorded a PSR of 0.980.
  • No variant reached the 0.95 DSR threshold; the best reported DSR was 0.822.
  • Placebo test: SMA p = 0.003; breakout p = 0.070.
  • The seven-state model's seed agreement was limited (ARI = 0.634).
Interpretation: The findings suggest potential value as a downside-risk filter, particularly for the SMA strategy. They do not establish a reliable trading edge: buy-and-hold remained stronger, and the DSR and stability checks warrant caution.
04 · Limitations & collaboration

Context matters

  • The study focused on one index, one historical period and two trend-following strategies.
  • The S&P 500 cash index is not directly tradable and excludes dividends.
  • Transaction costs were simplified and did not fully represent market impact, borrowing costs or all execution frictions.
  • Hidden states are statistical estimates, not verified economic labels.
  • Model stability was imperfect, with ARI = 0.634 for the seven-state model.
